Create, manage, and collaborate on your LinkedIn content from anywhere.

The Scripe Mobile App brings the core Scripe experience to your iPhone, making it easy to capture ideas the moment inspiration strikes, review content on the go, and stay connected with your workspace.

## Installing Scripe on iPhone

The Scripe Mobile App is available for iPhone through the Apple App Store.

<Tip>
  [You can download it here](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/scripe-mobile/id6757432825?l=en-GB).
</Tip>

Once installed, simply sign in with your existing Scripe account. Your workspaces, personal brands, drafts, and chats will automatically sync across desktop and mobile.

### **Navigating the App**

The Scripe Mobile App is organized into four main areas, making it easy to switch between capturing ideas, managing content, collaborating with your team, and creating new posts.

<Frame>
  ![Mobile App Uni 1](/images/mobile-app-uni-1.jpg "Mobile App Uni 1")
</Frame>

#### **Notes**

The **Notes** tab is your home screen and the best place to capture ideas as they come to you.

Use Notes to:

- Save ideas before you’re ready to turn them into posts
- Organize your thoughts in one place
- Edit existing notes
- Archive notes you no longer need

Whenever you’re ready, you can easily use a note as the starting point for a LinkedIn post.

#### **Posts**

The **Posts** tab gives you a complete overview of all your LinkedIn content.

Posts are organized by their current stage in your workflow. Simply tap any section to view, edit, or manage the posts inside it.

If you manage multiple personal brands, you can switch between them from the top of the screen.

#### **Inbox**

The **Inbox** keeps you updated on activity across your workspace such as review requests, comments, campaign activity, etc.

#### **Chat (Create Posts)**

The floating **Chat** button is where you create new content with Scripe. Tap the button to open a new conversation and choose how you’d like to create your post.

Available modes include:

- **Quick Post:** Generate a complete LinkedIn post from a simple idea.
- **Guided:** Answer a series of questions and let Scripe build your post step by step.
- **Ideation:** Brainstorm new topics, hooks, and content angles before writing.

Once you’ve selected a mode, simply type your prompt or use the microphone to dictate your ideas.

From the chat you can also click \+ to upload new files or files from your Knowledge Base as context.

## Installing Scripe on Mac Desktop

While the Scripe mobile app is perfect for capturing ideas and managing content on the go, many creators prefer doing longer writing sessions on a larger screen.

If you’re using a Mac, you can install Scripe as a desktop app directly from your browser.

1. Open [scripe.io](http://scripe.io) in your preferred browser (Safari or Chrome).
2. In the browser's address bar, look for the **Install** icon (a screen with a downward arrow).
3. Click the **Install** icon and confirm the installation.

Scripe will now be installed as an app on your Mac, accessible directly from your Applications folder or Dock.
